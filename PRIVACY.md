# Liemo privacy policy

Liemo is a video editor for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no
tracking, and there is no Liemo server: your work stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Projects, imported photos, videos and audio, recordings and exports.** Liemo keeps them in its
  own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Photos.** Liemo only sees the photos and videos you pick. When you export, the finished video is
  saved to your photo library, with your permission.
- **Microphone.** Used only while you record a voiceover. The recording is saved in your project.

## When something leaves your device

- **Auto captions (Mac, iPhone and iPad)** use Apple's speech recognition. On devices that support it,
  this runs entirely on the device; otherwise the audio is processed by Apple under
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Liemo AI is optional.** On iPhone and iPad it works only after you add your own Anthropic API
  key, which is kept in your device's Keychain. When you ask it for an edit, your message and the
  details of the open project (clip names, timings, text, and frames of the video when it checks its
  work) are sent to Anthropic's API under your account, governed by
  [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). On the Mac, Liemo AI uses the
  AI command-line tools you've installed and signed in to yourself, under their own terms.
- **Updates (Mac).** The Mac app checks GitHub for new versions of Liemo.
