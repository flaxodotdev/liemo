# BRIEF

**Spec:** `1920x1080@30, 22s, pace: calm, format: launch, audio: licensed track + launch kit`

**Name:** Liemo

**Product:** Liemo is a native, open-source video editor for Mac. You edit on a frame-accurate timeline, or an MCP client edits with you. While the app is open, those edits land live on the timeline, and every one can be undone. The editor is GPU-accelerated and written in Rust. There is no account and no Liemo server. Footage stays on disk. Exports go to Downloads. It is free and MIT licensed.

**Viewer:** people who edit video, and people who already use a coding agent, watching a launch post.

**Supplied:** a brand kit. The mark is the app icon's two white shards (`logo-mark.svg`, the same paths as the site logo). Colours: canvas `#0a0a0a`, paper `#ffffff`, ink `#f2f2f2`, muted `#8c8c8c`, hairline `#1f1f1f`, accent `#0099ff` (the editor's selection blue). The marketing site is a light paper page. The wordmark is Source Serif 4. Body type in the product is Inter. Tool names use JetBrains Mono.

**Brand exceptions:** the brand supplies a serif wordmark (Source Serif 4). Play the film on the dark ground: the accent `#0099ff` holds 2.7:1 on white paper, so it stays on the dark stage and on large graphics. Do not use the site's green status dot as a second accent.

**Case:** one request, in the user's words: "Cut a coast reel called Summer, slowly." The cut adds the coastal cliffs clip, a dissolve, the golden-hour look, and the title "Summer, slowly". The result is that frame in the preview.
